Step 3 · See the numbers

Farming in four pictures

Big numbers are easier to remember when you can see them. Press each button to fill the picture.

70 in 100

In developing countries, about 70 out of every 100 people depend on farming for their living.

2 out of 3
🧑‍🌾🧑‍🌾👩‍💼

In the whole world, nearly two-thirds of all workers are in farming. That's why it's called the most important occupation of man.

1 quarter

About one quarter (1 out of 4 parts) of all the land on Earth is farmed.

₹18 in ₹100

For every ₹100 India earns by selling things to other countries, about ₹18 comes from farm goods like jute, spices, cotton, tea and coffee.

One more thing from the book: places like India, China, Brazil, Europe and North America now grow much more on the same land, thanks to better seeds, fertilisers, pesticides and organic manure. You'll meet this again in Case 8.

What Shreshta learned today

  • Farming gives people their livelihood, gives us food, and gives animals fodder.
  • Factories need farms for raw material: cotton, jute, sugarcane, oilseeds.
  • Farm goods are sold abroad as exports and earn foreign exchange.
  • Farming creates employment, keeps trains and trucks busy, adds to national income and gives food security.
  • Agri, horti, seri, viti, pisci: fields, gardens, silk, grapes and fish.
