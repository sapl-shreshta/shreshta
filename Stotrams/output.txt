శ్రీ మహిషాసుర మర్దినీ స్తోత్రం (అయిగిరి నందిని)
అయి గిరినందిని నందితమేదిని విశ్వవినోదిని నందినుతేగిరివరవింధ్యశిరోధినివాసిని విష్ణువిలాసిని జిష్ణునుతే ।భగవతి హే శితికంఠకుటుంబిని భూరికుటుంబిని భూరికృతేజయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 1 ॥

Translation
1. Mother Durga – The Daughter of the Mountain and Joy of the WorldO daughter of the mountain, who makes the whole earth happy, who makes the whole universe rejoice, praised by Nandin. Dwelling on the peak of the great Vindhya mountain, glittering widely, (variation? giver of joy to Vishnu), praised by those desirous of victory. O Goddess, wife of the blue necked Siva, One who has many families, One who has done a lot, be victorious, be victorious, O destroyer of the demon mahisa, with beautiful braids of hair, daughter of the mountain Himalaya.
अयि (ayi) – O; गिरिनन्दिनि (girinandini) – daughter of the mountain (giri:mountain | nandini:daughter); नन्दितमेदिनि (nanditamedini) – who makes the earth happy (nandita:made happy | medini:earth); विश्वविनोदिनि (viśvavinodini) – who makes the universe rejoice (viśva:universe | vinodini:who makes rejoice); नन्दनुते (nandanute) – praised by Nandin (nanda:Nandin | nute:praised by); गिरिवरविन्ध्यशिरोधिनिवासिनि (girivaravindhyaśirodhinivāsini) – dwelling on Vindhya mountain peak (girivara:great mountain | vindhya:Vindhya | śirodhi:peak | nivāsini:dwelling); विष्णुविलासिनि (viṣṇuvilāsini) – giver of joy to Vishnu (viṣṇu:Vishnu | vilāsini:giver of joy); जिष्णुनुते (jiṣṇunute) – praised by the victorious (jiṣṇu:victorious | nute:praised by); भगवति (bhagavati) – O Goddess; हे (he) – O; शितिकण्ठकुटुम्बिनि (śitikaṇṭhakuṭumbini) – wife of blue-necked Siva (śitikaṇṭha:blue-necked | kuṭumbini:wife); भूरिकुटुम्बिनि (bhūrikuṭumbini) – one with many families (bhūri:many | kuṭumbini:families); भूरिकृते (bhūrikṛte) – one who has done a lot (bhūri:a lot | kṛte:done); जय (jaya) – victory; जय (jaya) – victory; हे (he) – O; महिषासुरमर्दिनि (mahiṣāsuramardini) – destroyer of demon Mahisa (mahiṣa:Mahisa | asura:demon | mardini:destroyer); रम्यकपर्दिनि (ramyakapardini) – with beautiful braids (ramya:beautiful | kapardini:braids); शैलसुते (śailasute) – daughter of the mountain (śaila:mountain | sute:daughter);
సురవరవర్షిణి దుర్ధరధర్షిణి దుర్ముఖమర్షిణి హర్షరతేత్రిభువనపోషిణి శంకరతోషిణి కల్మషమోషిణి ఘోరరతే । [కిల్బిష-, ఘోష-]దనుజనిరోషిణి దితిసుతరోషిణి దుర్మదశోషిణి సింధుసుతేజయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 2 ॥

2. Mother Durga – Destroys the Danavas and the Daityas and Nourishes the Three WorldsO bestower of boons on Gods, One who assails those hard to control, who tolerates those with ugly faces (?), one engrossed in rejoicing . One who nourishes the three worlds, One who pleases sankara, One who removes sins, One who engrosses in sound of Om (?) . One who is angry with the progeny of Danu (demon), One who is angry with the sons of Diti (also demon), One who destroys those with evil intoxication of pride, daughter of the ocean.
सुरवरवर्षिणि (suravaravarṣiṇi) – bestower of boons on gods (sura:god | vara:boon | varṣiṇi:bestower); दुर्धरधर्षिणि (durdharadharṣiṇi) – assails those hard to control (durdhara:hard to control | dharṣiṇi:assails); दुर्मुखमर्षिणि (durmukhamarṣiṇi) – tolerates those with ugly faces (durmukha:ugly faces | marṣiṇi:tolerates); हर्षरते (harṣarate) – engrossed in rejoicing (harṣa:rejoicing | rate:engrossed in); त्रिभुवनपोषिणि (tribhuvanapoṣiṇi) – nourishes the three worlds (tri:three | bhuvana:worlds | poṣiṇi:nourishes); शङ्करतोषिणि (śaṅkaratoṣiṇi) – pleases Śaṅkara (śaṅkara:Śaṅkara | toṣiṇi:pleases); किल्बिषमोषिणि (kilbiṣamoṣiṇi) – removes sins (kilbiṣa:sins | moṣiṇi:removes); घोषरते (ghoṣarate) – engrossed in sound (ghoṣa:sound | rate:engrossed in); दनुजनिरोषिणि (danujaniroṣiṇi) – angry with progeny of Danu (danuja:progeny of Danu | niroṣiṇi:angry with); दितिसुतरोषिणि (ditisutaroṣiṇi) – angry with sons of Diti (diti:Diti | suta:sons | roṣiṇi:angry with); दुर्मदशोषिणि (durmadaśoṣiṇi) – destroys evil intoxication of pride (durmada:evil intoxication of pride | śoṣiṇi:destroys); सिन्धुसुते (sindhusute) – daughter of the ocean (sindhu:ocean | sute:daughter); जय (jaya) – victory; जय (jaya) – victory; हे (he) – O; महिषासुरमर्दिनि (mahiṣāsuramardini) – slayer of Mahiṣāsura (mahiṣāsura:Mahiṣāsura | mardini:slayer); रम्यकपर्दिनि (ramyakapardini) – beautifully braided (ramya:beautiful | kapardini:braided); शैलसुते (śailasute) – daughter of the mountain (śaila:mountain | sute:daughter);
అయి జగదంబ మదంబ కదంబవనప్రియవాసిని హాసరతేశిఖరి శిరోమణి తుంగహిమాలయ శృంగనిజాలయ మధ్యగతే ।మధుమధురే మధుకైటభగంజిని కైటభభంజిని రాసరతేజయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 3 ॥

3. Mother Durga – Destroyer of the Demons Madhu and KaitabhaO mother of the world, my mother, One who loves to dwell in a forest of Kadamba trees, One who keeps on smiling . One who is on her own dwelling on the tall peak of the Himalaya, the greatest among the mountains . One who is very sweet like honey, One who has the treasure of demons Madhu and Kaitabha, destroyer of the demon Kaitabha, engaged in dancing.
अयि (ayi) – O; जगदम्ब (jagadamba) – mother of the world (jagat:world | amba:mother); मदम्ब (madamba) – my mother (mad:my | amba:mother); कदम्बवनप्रियवासिनि (kadambavanapriyavāsini) – one who loves Kadamba forest (kadamba:Kadamba | vana:forest | priya:beloved | vāsini:dweller); हासरते (hāsarate) – engaged in smiling; शिखरिशिरोमणि (śikhariśiromaṇi) – crest jewel of peaks (śikhari:peak | śiraḥ:head | maṇi:jewel); तुङ्गहिमालय (tuṅgahimālaya) – tall Himalaya (tuṅga:tall | himālaya:Himalaya); श‍ृङ्गनिजालय (śṛṅganijālaya) – dwelling on the peak (śṛṅga:peak | nija:own | ālaya:dwelling); मध्यगते (madhyagate) – situated in the middle; मधुमधुरे (madhumadhure) – sweet like honey (madhu:honey | madhura:sweet); मधुकैटभगञ्जिनि (madhukaiṭabhagañjini) – destroyer of Madhu and Kaitabha (madhu:Madhu | kaiṭabha:Kaitabha | gañjini:destroyer); कैटभभञ्जिनि (kaiṭabhabhañjini) – destroyer of Kaitabha (kaiṭabha:Kaitabha | bhañjini:destroyer); रासरते (rāsarate) – engaged in dancing; जय (jaya) – victory; जय (jaya) – victory; हे (he) – O; महिषासुरमर्दिनि (mahiṣāsuramardini) – destroyer of Mahishasura (mahiṣa:Mahisha | asura:demon | mardini:destroyer); रम्यकपर्दिनि (ramyakapardini) – beautifully braided (ramya:beautiful | kapardini:braided); शैलसुते (śailasute) – daughter of the mountain (śaila:mountain | sute:daughter);
అయి శతఖండ విఖండితరుండ వితుండితశుండ గజాధిపతేరిపుగజగండ విదారణచండ పరాక్రమశుండ మృగాధిపతే ।నిజభుజదండ నిపాతితఖండ విపాతితముండ భటాధిపతే [-చండ]జయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 4 ॥
ayi śatakhaṇḍa vikhaṇḍitaruṇḍa vituṇḍitaśuṇḍa gajādhipateripugajagaṇḍa vidāraṇacaṇḍa parākramaśuṇḍa mṛgādhipate .nijabhujadaṇḍa nipātitakhaṇḍa vipātitamuṇḍa bhaṭādhipatejaya jaya he mahiṣāsuramardini ramyakapardini śailasute .. 4 ..
4. Mother Durga – Destroyer of the Demons Chanda and MundaO One who split the heads (of demons) into hundreds of pieces and One who cut the trunks of great battle elephants . whose great lion is skilled in terrifying valor in tearing apart the temples of enemy elephants . One who has cut down into pieces the heads of enemy chieftains with the strength of her own arms.
अयि (ayi) – O; शतखण्ड (śatakhaṇḍa) – hundred pieces (śata:hundred | khaṇḍa:pieces); विखण्डितरुण्ड (vikhaṇḍitaruṇḍa) – split heads (vikhaṇḍita:split | ruṇḍa:heads); वितुण्डितशुण्ड (vituṇḍitaśuṇḍa) – cut trunks (vituṇḍita:cut | śuṇḍa:trunks); गजाधिपते (gajādhipate) – O lord of elephants (gaja:elephant | adhipate:O lord); रिपुगजगण्ड (ripugajagaṇḍa) – enemy elephant temples (ripu:enemy | gaja:elephant | gaṇḍa:temples); विदारणचण्ड (vidāraṇacaṇḍa) – terrifying valor (vidāraṇa:tearing apart | caṇḍa:terrifying); पराक्रमशुण्ड (parākramaśuṇḍa) – valor trunk (parākrama:valor | śuṇḍa:trunk); मृगाधिपते (mṛgādhipate) – O lord of beasts (mṛga:beast | adhipate:O lord); निजभुजदण्ड (nijabhujadaṇḍa) – own arm strength (nija:own | bhuja:arm | daṇḍa:strength); निपातितखण्ड (nipātitakhaṇḍa) – cut into pieces (nipātita:cut | khaṇḍa:pieces); विपातितमुण्ड (vipātitamuṇḍa) – severed heads (vipātita:severed | muṇḍa:heads); भटाधिपते (bhaṭādhipate) – O lord of warriors (bhaṭa:warrior | adhipate:O lord); जय (jaya) – victory; जय (jaya) – victory; हे (he) – O; महिषासुरमर्दिनि (mahiṣāsuramardini) – destroyer of Mahishasura (mahiṣa:Mahisha | asura:demon | mardini:destroyer); रम्यकपर्दिनि (ramyakapardini) – beautifully braided (ramya:beautiful | kapardini:braided); शैलसुते (śailasute) – daughter of the mountain (śaila:mountain | sute:daughter);
అయి రణదుర్మద శత్రువధోదిత దుర్ధరనిర్జర శక్తిభృతేచతురవిచారధురీణ మహాశివ దూతకృత ప్రమథాధిపతే ।దురితదురీహ దురాశయ దుర్మతి దానవదూత కృతాంతమతేజయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 5 ॥
ayi raṇadurmada śatruvadhodita durdharanirjara śaktibhṛtecaturavicāra dhurīṇamahāśiva dūtakṛta pramathādhipate .duritadurīha durāśayadurmati dānavaduta kṛtāntamatejaya jaya he mahiṣāsuramardini ramyakapardini śailasute .. 5 ..
5. Mother Durga – Made Lord Shiva the Messenger against enemies. O One who holds the invincible and undiminishing striking force which arose on the occasion of killing the enemies who were hard to subdue on the battlefield . who made Pramatha, the great attendant of Shiva, a leader in subtle thinking, her commander (?) . who decided to destroy the messenger of demons who were sinful, with evil intentions, thoughts and mind.
अयि (ayi) – O; रणदुर्मद (raṇadurmada) – battle arrogance (raṇa:battle | durmada:arrogance); शत्रुवधोदित (śatruvadhodita) – arisen for enemy's killing (śatru:enemy | vadha:killing | udita:arisen); दुर्धरनिर्जर (durdharanirjara) – invincible and undiminishing (durdhara:invincible | nirjara:undiminishing); शक्तिभृते (śaktibhṛte) – holder of power (śakti:power | bhṛte:holder); चतुरविचार (caturavicāra) – subtle thinking (catura:subtle | vicāra:thinking); धुरीणमहाशिव (dhurīṇamahāśiva) – great attendant of Shiva (dhurīṇa:attendant | mahāśiva:great Shiva); दूतकृत (dūtakṛta) – made messenger (dūta:messenger | kṛta:made); प्रमथाधिपते (pramathādhipate) – lord of Pramatha (pramatha:Pramatha | adhipate:lord); दुरितदुरीह (duritadurīha) – sinful intentions (durita:sinful | durīha:intentions); दुराशयदुर्मति (durāśayadurmati) – evil thoughts and mind (durāśaya:evil thoughts | durmati:evil mind); दानवदुत (dānavaduta) – demon's messenger (dānava:demon | duta:messenger); कृतान्तमते (kṛtāntamate) – decided to destroy (kṛtānta:destroy | mate:decided); जय (jaya) – victory; जय (jaya) – victory; हे (he) – O; महिषासुरमर्दिनि (mahiṣāsuramardini) – slayer of Mahishasura (mahiṣāsura:Mahishasura | mardini:slayer); रम्यकपर्दिनि (ramyakapardini) – beautifully braided (ramya:beautiful | kapardini:braided); शैलसुते (śailasute) – daughter of the mountain (śaila:mountain | sute:daughter);
అయి శరణాగత వైరివధూవర వీరవరాభయదాయకరేత్రిభువన మస్తక శూలవిరోధి శిరోధికృతామల శూలకరే ।దుమిదుమితామర దుందుభినాద మహో ముఖరీకృత తిగ్మకరేజయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 6 ॥

6. Mother Durga – Pardons the Enemy Heroes when their Wives take RefugeO One who gives protection to the great heroic husbands of the enemy wives who have come seeking refuge . One who holds in her hands a spotless spear pointed towards the head of the opponent who is causing a great pain for all the three worlds . One who is like the blazing hot sun, aroused by the power of resounding noise of the drums of Gods.
अयि (ayi) – O; शरणागत (śaraṇāgata) – seeking refuge; वैरिवधुवर (vairivadhuvara) – enemy wives' great husbands (vaira:enemy | vadhu:wives | vara:great husbands); वीरवराभय (vīravarābhaya) – protection to great heroes (vīra:heroes | vara:great | abhaya:protection); दायकरे (dāyakare) – O giver; त्रिभुवनमस्तक (tribhuvanamastaka) – heads of the three worlds (tri:three | bhuvana:worlds | mastaka:heads); शुलविरोधि (śulavirodhi) – opponent of the spear (śula:spear | virodhi:opponent); शिरोऽधिकृत (śiro'dhikṛta) – head of the opponent (śiraḥ:head | adhikṛta:of the opponent); अमल (amala) – spotless; शुलकरे (śulakare) – holder of the spear (śula:spear | kare:holder); दुमिदुमितामर (dumidumitāmara) – resounding noise of the gods (dumi:resounding | dumita:noise | amara:gods); धुन्दुभिनादमहोमुखरीकृत (dhundubhinādamahomukharīkṛta) – resounding with drum noise (dhundubhi:drum | nāda:noise | maha:great | mukhara:resounding | īkṛta:made); दिङ्मकरे (diṅmakare) – sun of directions (diṅ:directions | makara:sun); जय (jaya) – victory; जय (jaya) – victory; हे (he) – O; महिषासुरमर्दिनि (mahiṣāsuramardini) – slayer of Mahiṣāsura (mahiṣa:buffalo | asura:demon | mardini:slayer); रम्यकपर्दिनि (ramyakapardini) – beautifully braided (ramya:beautiful | kapardini:braided); शैलसुते (śailasute) – daughter of the mountain (śaila:mountain | sute:daughter);
అయి నిజహుంకృతిమాత్ర నిరాకృత ధూమ్రవిలోచన ధూమ్రశతేసమరవిశోషిత శోణితబీజ సముద్భవశోణిత బీజలతే ।శివ శివ శుంభ నిశుంభ మహాహవ తర్పిత భూత పిశాచరతేజయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 7 ॥

Mother Durga – Whose Pure Moon-Like Face Subdues our ImpuritiesOh Pure One, the person who looks upon your moonlike face together with its digits causing a bond (with your essence) as the bank (beyond which the river of sorrow does not proceed) - will he ever be turned away by the ladies of Indra's City, having moonlike faces and auspicious faces ? Oh Goddess, having the name of Siva as her treasure, or my opinion is that with kindness you achieve it. Oh Goddess, who has crushed the demon, Mahisha, wearing a beatiful know of hair, Oh Daughter of Mountain (Himalaya), Victory to you, Victory to you.
