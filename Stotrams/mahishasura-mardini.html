Mahishasura Mardini Stotram

Prayer to Mother Durga, destroyer of demons 🙏

📍 VERSE 1
అయి గిరినందిని నందితమేదిని విశ్వవినోదిని నందినుతే గిరివరవింధ్యశిరోధినివాసిని విష్ణువిలాసిని జిష్ణునుతే । భగవతి హే శితికంఠకుటుంబిని భూరికుటుంబిని భూరికృతే జయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 1 ॥
ayi girinandini nanditamedini viśvavinodini nandanute girivaravindhyaśirodhinivāsini viṣṇuvilāsini jiṣṇunute . bhagavati he śitikaṇṭhakuṭumbini bhūrikuṭumbini bhūrikṛte jaya jaya he mahiṣāsuramardini ramyakapardini śailasute .. 1..
Word-by-Word Meaning:

అయి (ayi) – O | గిరినందిని (girinandini) – daughter of the mountain (giri: mountain | nandini: daughter)

నందితమేదిని (nanditamedini) – who makes the earth happy (nandita: made happy | medini: earth)

విశ్వవినోదిని (viśvavinodini) – who makes the universe rejoice (viśva: universe | vinodini: makes rejoice)

నందనుతే (nandanute) – praised by Nandin (nanda: Nandin | nute: praised by)

గిరివరవింధ్యశిరోధినివాసిని (girivaravindhyaśirodhinivāsini) – dwelling on Vindhya mountain peak (girivara: great mountain | vindhya: Vindhya | śirodhi: peak | nivāsini: dwelling)

విష్ణువిలాసిని (viṣṇuvilāsini) – giver of joy to Vishnu (viṣṇu: Vishnu | vilāsini: giver of joy)

జిష్ణునుతే (jiṣṇunute) – praised by the victorious (jiṣṇu: victorious | nute: praised by)

భగవతి (bhagavati) – O Goddess | హే (he) – O

శితికంఠకుటుంబిని (śitikaṇṭhakuṭumbini) – wife of blue-necked Siva (śitikaṇṭha: blue-necked | kuṭumbini: wife)

భూరికుటుంబిని (bhūrikuṭumbini) – one with many families (bhūri: many | kuṭumbini: families)

భూరికృతే (bhūrikṛte) – one who has done a lot (bhūri: a lot | kṛte: done)

జయ జయ (jaya jaya) – victory, victory | హే (he) – O

మహిషాసురమర్దిని (mahiṣāsuramardini) – destroyer of demon Mahisa (mahiṣa: Mahisa | asura: demon | mardini: destroyer)

రమ్యకపర్దిని (ramyakapardini) – with beautiful braids (ramya: beautiful | kapardini: braids)

శైలసుతే (śailasute) – daughter of the mountain (śaila: mountain | sute: daughter)

Overall Meaning:
Mother Durga – The Daughter of the Mountain and Joy of the World

O daughter of the mountain, who makes the whole earth happy, who makes the whole universe rejoice, praised by Nandin. Dwelling on the peak of the great Vindhya mountain, glittering widely, (variation? giver of joy to Vishnu), praised by those desirous of victory. O Goddess, wife of the blue necked Siva, One who has many families, One who has done a lot, be victorious, be victorious, O destroyer of the demon mahisa, with beautiful braids of hair, daughter of the mountain Himalaya.

📍 VERSE 2
సురవరవర్షిణి దుర్ధరధర్షిణి దుర్ముఖమర్షిణి హర్షరతే త్రిభువనపోషిణి శంకరతోషిణి కల్మషమోషిణి ఘోரரతే । దనుజనిరోషిణి దితిసుతరోషిణి దుర్మదశోషిణి సింధుసుతే జయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 2 ॥
Suravaravarṣiṇi durdharadharṣiṇi durmukhamarṣiṇi harṣarate tribhuvanapoṣiṇi śaṅkaratoṣiṇi kilbiṣamoṣiṇi ghoṣarate. Danujaniroṣiṇi ditisutaroṣiṇi durmadaśoṣiṇi sindhusute jaya jaya he mahiṣāsuramardini ramyakapardini śailasute.. 2..
Word-by-Word Meaning:

సురవరవర్షిణి (suravaravarṣiṇi) – bestower of boons on gods (sura: god | vara: boon | varṣiṇi: bestower)

దుర్ధరధర్షిణి (durdharadharṣiṇi) – assails those hard to control (durdhara: hard to control | dharṣiṇi: assails)

దుర్ముఖమర్షిణి (durmukhamarṣiṇi) – tolerates those with ugly faces (durmukha: ugly faces | marṣiṇi: tolerates)

హర్షరతే (harṣarate) – engrossed in rejoicing (harṣa: rejoicing | rate: engrossed in)

త్రిభువనపోషిణి (tribhuvanapoṣiṇi) – nourishes the three worlds (tri: three | bhuvana: worlds | poṣiṇi: nourishes)

శంకరతోషిణి (śaṅkaratoṣiṇi) – pleases Śaṅkara (śaṅkara: Śaṅkara | toṣiṇi: pleases)

కల్మషమోషిణి (kilbiṣamoṣiṇi) – removes sins (kilbiṣa: sins | moṣiṇi: removes)

ఘోరరతే (ghoṣarate) – engrossed in sound (ghoṣa: sound | rate: engrossed in)

దనుజనిరోషిణి (danujaniroṣiṇi) – angry with progeny of Danu (danuja: progeny of Danu | niroṣiṇi: angry with)

దితిసుతరోషిణి (ditisutaroṣiṇi) – angry with sons of Diti (diti: Diti | suta: sons | roṣiṇi: angry with)

దుర్మదశోషిణి (durmadaśoṣiṇi) – destroys evil intoxication of pride (durmada: evil intoxication of pride | śoṣiṇi: destroys)

సింధుసుతే (sindhusute) – daughter of the ocean (sindhu: ocean | sute: daughter)

జయ జయ హే (jaya jaya he) – victory, victory, O | మహిషాసురమర్దిని (mahiṣāsuramardini) – slayer of Mahiṣāsura

రమ్యకపర్దిని (ramyakapardini) – beautifully braided (ramya: beautiful | kapardini: braided)

శైలసుతే (śailasute) – daughter of the mountain (śaila: mountain | sute: daughter)

Overall Meaning:
Mother Durga – Destroys the Danavas and the Daityas and Nourishes the Three Worlds

O bestower of boons on Gods, One who assails those hard to control, who tolerates those with ugly faces. One who nourishes the three worlds, One who pleases Sankara, One who removes sins. One who is angry with the progeny of Danu, One who is angry with the sons of Diti, One who destroys those with evil intoxication of pride, daughter of the ocean.

📍 VERSE 3

Mother Durga – Destroyer of the Demons Madhu and Kaitabha

O mother of the world, my mother, One who loves to dwell in a forest of Kadamba trees, One who keeps on smiling. One who dwells on the tall peak of the Himalaya, the greatest among the mountains. One who is very sweet like honey, destroyer of the demon Kaitabha, engaged in dancing.

📍 VERSE 5
అయి రణదుర్మద శత్రువధోదిత దుర్ధరనిర్జర శక్తిభృతే చతురవిచారధురీణ మహాశివ దూతకృత ప్రమథాధిపతే । దురితదురీహ దురాశయ దుర్మతి దానవదూత కృతాంతమతే జయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే ॥ 5 ॥

One who holds in her hands a spotless spear pointed towards the head of the opponent who is causing a great pain for all the three worlds . One who is like the blazing hot sun, aroused by the power of resounding noise of the drums of Gods.
