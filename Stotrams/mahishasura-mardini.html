Mahishasura Mardini Stotram

Prayer to Mother Durga, destroyer of demons 🙏

📍 VERSE 1
అయి గిరినందిని నందితమేదిని విశ్ fregu వినోదిని నందినుతేగిరివరవింధ్యశిరోధినివాసిని విష్ణువిలాసిని జిష్ణునుతే ।భగవతి హే శితికంఠకుటుంబిని భూరికుటుంబిని భూరికృతేజయ జయ హే మహిషాసురమర్దిని రమ్యకపర్దిని శైలసుతే
Ayi girinandini nanditamedini viśvavinodini nandanute girivaravindhyaśirodhinivāsini viṣṇuvilāsini jiṣṇunute. Bhagavati he śitikaṇṭhakuṭumbini bhūrikuṭumbini bhūrikṛte jaya jaya he mahiṣāsuramardini ramyakapardini śailasute.
Word-by-Word Meaning:

అయి (ayi) – O | గిరినందిని (girinandini) – daughter of the mountain (giri: mountain | nandini: daughter)

నందితమేదిని (nanditamedini) – who makes the earth happy (nandita: made happy | medini: earth)

విశ్వవినోదిని (viśvavinodini) – who makes the universe rejoice (viśva: universe | vinodini: who makes rejoice)

విష్ణువిలాసిని (viṣṇuvilāsini) – giver of joy to Vishnu (viṣṇu: Vishnu | vilāsini: giver of joy)

Overall Meaning:
O daughter of the mountain, who makes the whole earth happy, who makes the whole universe rejoice, praised by Nandin. Dwelling on the peak of the great Vindhya mountain, glittering widely, giver of joy to Vishnu, praised by those desirous of victory. O Goddess, wife of the blue-necked Siva, One who has many families, One who has done a lot, be victorious, be victorious, O destroyer of the demon Mahisa, with beautiful braids of hair, daughter of the mountain Himalaya.
📍 VERSE 2

Word-by-Word Meaning:

సురవరవర్షిణి (suravaravarṣiṇi) – bestower of boons on gods (sura: god | vara: boon | varṣiṇi: bestower)

దుర్ముఖమర్షిణి (durmukhamarṣiṇi) – tolerates those with ugly faces (durmukha: ugly faces | marṣiṇi: tolerates)

త్రిభువనపోషిణి (tribhuvanapoṣiṇi) – nourishes the three worlds (tri: three | bhuvana: worlds | poṣiṇi: nourishes)

శంకరతోషిణి (śaṅkaratoṣiṇi) – pleases Śaṅkara (śaṅkara: Śaṅkara | toṣiṇi: pleases)

కల్మషమోషిణి (kilbiṣamoṣiṇi) – removes sins (kilbiṣa: sins | moṣiṇi: removes)

Overall Meaning:
O bestower of boons on Gods, One who assails those hard to control, who tolerates those with ugly faces. One who nourishes the three worlds, One who pleases Sankara, One who removes sins. One who is angry with the progeny of Danu (demon), One who is angry with the sons of Diti (also demon), One who destroys those with evil intoxication of pride, daughter of the ocean.

📍 VERSE 6
