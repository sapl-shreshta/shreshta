👆 Tap a number to see examples!

💡 Remember: Only 1, 2, 3 and 4 change with gender.
From 5 (पञ्च) onwards, the number is the same for all three genders — पञ्च बालकाः, पञ्च बालिकाः, पञ्च फलानि.
🔟 11–19 are made by adding a number before दश (एक + दश = एकादश, षट् + दश = षोडश). 20 = विंशतिः.
✨ 2 (द्वे) is the same for feminine and neuter: द्वे बालिके, द्वे फले.
