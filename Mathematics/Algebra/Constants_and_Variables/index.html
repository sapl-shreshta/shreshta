🦎 Meet Variable Vee

🧩 Here's a puzzle: Your mom is always 30 years older than you. How do we write a math rule for that — one that works this year, next year, and every year after?

If your age were always fixed, say 8, we could just write 30 + 8 = 38 and be done. But your age keeps changing every single year! So one fixed number can't do the job — we need a symbol that can stand for whatever your age happens to be.

That's exactly what a variable is for. Let's say x = your age. Then:

Mom's age = 30 + x

As x (your age) changes, 30 + x (mom's age) automatically changes right along with it — one rule works forever!

🦎

Vee the Shape-Shifting Chameleon can be any color, any size — it keeps changing! In algebra, a letter like x, y, or a works the same way: it stands for a number that can change, or one we don't know yet.

A VARIABLE is a letter that stands for a number that can change or is unknown. Its value is not fixed.

Try it yourself! Slide to change your age (x) and watch mom's age (30 + x) update instantly:

Your age (x) = 8
Mom's age (30 + x) = 38

🤝 Why Algebra Needs BOTH

Imagine a pencil shop. Every pencil costs ₹5 — that price never changes, so it's a constant. But how many pencils you buy can be anything — that's your variable, let's call it x.

Total cost = 5 × x   (constant × variable)

Slide to pick how many pencils you want to buy:

5 × 1 = ₹5

📝 Quick Recap

  • 🪨 A constant is a value that always stays the same.
  • 🦎 A variable is a letter that stands for a number that can change or is unknown.
  • 🤝 Algebra combines both — constants keep things fixed, variables let us describe anything that changes.
  • 🌍 They show up everywhere: arithmetic, geometry formulas, science, and everyday life!
