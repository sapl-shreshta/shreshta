⚖️ Is It Balanced?

⚖️

An equation is a statement that says two expressions are EQUAL — joined by an "=" sign. The expression on the left is called the L.H.S. (Left Hand Side), and the one on the right is the R.H.S. (Right Hand Side).

If the L.H.S. and the R.H.S. are NOT actually equal in value, then it is NOT an equation!

Think of a balance scale. If both sides weigh the same, the scale stays level — that's an equation! If one side is heavier, the scale tips — that's NOT an equation.

L.H.S.: 4 + 2  vs  R.H.S.: 7 − 1
🎉 You've checked every scale! Great job spotting equations.

🔢 Numerical vs Algebraic Equation

A numerical equation contains only numbers — like 25 × 5 + 6 = 155. An algebraic equation contains one or more variables (letters) — like 3x = 12.

🧩 Parts of an Equation

Here's a brand-new word: the coefficient — the number that multiplies a variable. In 5x − 2 = 8, the coefficient is 5, because it multiplies the variable x.

5x − 2 = 8  →  coefficient = 5, variable = x, operator = −, constants = 2 and 8

🚫 What is NOT an Equation

Statements that use >, <, ≥, ≤ instead of "=" are called inequalities, not equations. For example, 2x − 3 > 4 is NOT an equation, but 2x − 3 = 1 IS an equation.

🔍 The Solution — Trial and Error

The solution of an equation is the value of the variable that makes the L.H.S. equal the R.H.S. We say that value satisfies the equation.

Equation: 2x + 4 = 10. Slide x and watch the L.H.S. change — find the value that matches the R.H.S.!
x = 1
L.H.S. = 2 × 1 + 4 = 6
R.H.S. = 10
✅ MATCH! x = 3 satisfies the equation!

📝 Quick Recap

  • ⚖️ An equation says the L.H.S. equals the R.H.S. — if the sides aren't equal, it's NOT an equation.
  • 🔢 Equations can be numerical (only numbers) or algebraic (with a variable).
  • 🧩 Parts of an equation: coefficient, variable, operator, and constant.
  • 🚫 Statements with >, <, ≥, ≤ are inequalities — NOT equations.
  • 🔍 The solution is the value that satisfies the equation (makes both sides equal).
  • 🚀 Trial and error works, but transposition (coming soon) finds solutions much faster!
