🍎 Like Terms vs Unlike Terms

🍎🍊

3 apples + 2 apples = 5 apples. Easy! They're both apples, so we can just add them together.

But what about 3 apples + 2 oranges? You can't combine those into one thing — they stay separate: 3 apples and 2 oranges. Apples and oranges just aren't the same kind of thing!

Algebra works exactly the same way. 3x and 7x are LIKE TERMS — they have the exact same letter (x), only the coefficient (the number in front) is different. We can combine them!
3x and 3y are UNLIKE TERMS — different letters. Just like apples and oranges, they have to stay separate.

🛍️ Two Quick Real-Life Examples

🛒 3 notebooks at ₹15 each + 2 more notebooks at ₹15 each = 5 notebooks at ₹15 each = ₹75 total. The notebooks are "like terms" — we just add how many there are!
⏱️ 20 minutes cooking today + 20 minutes cooking tomorrow = 40 minutes total cooking. Same kind of thing (cooking minutes), so they combine!

📝 Quick Recap

  • 🍎 Like terms have the exact same letter — only their coefficients can be added or subtracted.
  • 🍊 Unlike terms have different letters — they cannot be combined and stay separate.
  • 🧩 To simplify an expression: group the like terms, then add or subtract their coefficients.
