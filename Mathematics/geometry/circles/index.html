Circles

Round, perfectly balanced, and everywhere you look!

📐

Hi, Ray the Ruler here 👋 A circle looks simple, but it hides a beautiful rule: every single point on it is exactly the same distance from one special centre point. Let's drag things around and see it for ourselves!

🎯 Meet a Circle Drag the pink point!

A circle is the set of all points that are exactly the same distance from one fixed centre point (we call it O). That fixed distance is called the radius.

Drag the pink point anywhere — watch it snap right back onto the circle, always the same distance from O!

🕐

Real life: A clock face, a coin, a wheel, and a pizza are all circles — every point on their edge is the same distance from the middle!

🕵️ Interior, Exterior & On the Circle Click anywhere!

Every point in the picture is either inside the circle, outside it, or sitting exactly on the circle itself. Click anywhere and find out which!

👆 Click anywhere in the box above!

📏 Radius, Diameter & Chord Drag both dots!

A chord is a straight line segment joining any two points on a circle. Drag the two dots around the circle below — when the chord happens to pass exactly through the centre, it becomes the longest possible chord: a diameter! A diameter is always exactly 2 × radius long.

🔍 Remember: radius (O to the edge) is half a diameter. A diameter is a chord too — just a very special one that goes straight through the centre.

🍕 Arc, Sector & Segment Drag both dots!

Two radii split a circle into a major (bigger) and minor (smaller) sector — like slices of a pizza. A single chord instead splits it into a major and minor segment. Either way, the curved edge itself gets split into a major and minor arc. Switch tabs and drag the dots to see all three!

🍕

Real life: A slice of pizza is a sector, and the crust along its curved edge is an arc!

🌗 Semicircles & Concentric Circles

A semicircle is exactly half a circle, cut by a diameter. Concentric circles all share the same centre but have different radii — like ripples in a pond.

However you rotate the diameter, it always cuts the circle into two equal semicircles!

Just 1 circle so far.

📏 Tangent & Secant Lines Drag up/down!

A straight line can meet a circle in three different ways. Drag the blue line up and down and watch what happens!

📌 Fun fact: right at the point a tangent touches a circle, the radius drawn to that point is always perpendicular (90°) to the tangent line!

🥧 Circumference & Area Drag the edge!

Every circle has a distance all the way around (its circumference) and an amount of space inside it (its area) — both connected to the radius by a special number: π (pi) ≈ 3.14.

🔮 Fun fact: π (pi) is the SAME number for every circle in the universe, big or small — about 3.14159... and its digits never end or repeat!
