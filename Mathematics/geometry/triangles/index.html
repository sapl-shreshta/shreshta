🔺 Meet a Triangle Drag C!

A triangle is a polygon made of just 3 sides. It's the polygon with the fewest possible sides — you can't close up a shape with only 1 or 2 straight lines!

However you drag point C, this shape always has exactly 3 sides, 3 vertices, and 3 angles — the fewest a closed polygon can have!

🔍 Special case: a triangle has zero diagonals! A diagonal connects two non-adjacent vertices — but in a triangle, every vertex is already directly joined to both of the others by a side. There's no "non-adjacent pair" left to connect. Every other polygon (4+ sides) has at least one diagonal.

🔺

Real life: A slice of pizza, a road-work sign, and the Egyptian pyramids are all built from triangles!

🔮 Recap: Least sides of any polygon (3) & zero diagonals. Classify by sides (scalene/isosceles/equilateral) or by angles (acute/right/obtuse). The 3 interior angles always sum to 180°. An exterior angle always equals the sum of the two remote interior angles. Perimeter = sum of all 3 sides.
