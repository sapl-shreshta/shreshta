Hi, Ray the Ruler here again 👋 A quadrilateral is any closed shape with exactly 4 straight sides. Let's drag some corners around and discover the rules every quadrilateral follows — and meet the whole family of special quadrilateral shapes!

◻️ Meet a Quadrilateral Drag C and D!

A quadrilateral is a polygon with 4 sides, 4 vertices and 4 angles. Drag points C and D below and watch the sides and diagonals relabel themselves live.

However you drag C and D, this shape always keeps 4 sides, 4 vertices, 4 angles and exactly 2 diagonals (the dashed lines AC and BD)!

🔍 Diagonal: a line joining two vertices that are not already joined by a side. A quadrilateral has exactly 2 of them: AC and BD.
🪟

Real life: A window pane, a book cover, and a tablet screen are all everyday quadrilaterals!

🏷️ The Quadrilateral Family

Not all quadrilaterals are the same! Little marks tell us their special properties: matching arrows mean two sides are parallel, matching tick marks mean two sides are equal length, and a small square corner means a right angle (90°).
