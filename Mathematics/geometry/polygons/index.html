Polygons

Flat shapes made of straight sides — from triangles to decagons!

📐

Hi, Ray the Ruler here 👋 A polygon is any flat shape you can draw with a ruler without lifting it, ending exactly where you started, and never crossing your own line. Let's explore what makes a shape a polygon, and build some ourselves!

🔷 What Is a Polygon? Click a corner!

A polygon is a simple closed curve made only of straight line segments. "Simple" means the sides never cross each other, and "closed" means it joins back up to where it started. The corners are called vertices (one is a vertex), and the straight edges are called sides.

👆 Click any labelled corner to see which corners are its adjacent vertices (right next door) and which ones need a diagonal to reach!

🔍 Key words: Two vertices are adjacent if a single side joins them directly (like next-door neighbours). Two sides are adjacent if they share a vertex. A diagonal is a line segment that connects two non-adjacent vertices — it cuts across the inside of the polygon instead of running along the edge.
🔷

Real life: Honeycomb cells, stop signs, and soccer-ball panels are all polygons — hexagons and octagons hiding in plain sight!

🔍 Formulas: Interior angle sum = (n − 2) × 180°. For a regular polygon (equal angles), each interior angle = the sum ÷ n. The exterior angle sum is always 360° — no matter how many sides! Number of diagonals = n(n − 3) ÷ 2 (each of the n vertices connects to n − 3 others by a diagonal, and we divide by 2 so we don't count each diagonal twice). A triangle (n = 3) gets 0 diagonals — every vertex is already joined to both others by a side!

🎭 Regular vs Irregular, Convex vs Concave

These are two completely separate ways to describe a polygon — one is about its measurements, the other is about its shape.

📐 Regular vs Irregular (about measurements)

Regular: ALL sides are the same length AND all angles are the same size. Every corner and every edge matches exactly.

Irregular: the sides and/or angles are different sizes — even one mismatched side or angle is enough to make it irregular.

🧭 Convex vs Concave (about shape)

Convex: every corner points outward — if you drew a straight line between any two points inside the polygon, that line would always stay fully inside it.

Concave: at least one corner points inward, making a "dent" or arrow-like notch — a straight line between two points inside it can poke outside the shape.

➰ Simple vs Self-Intersecting

A true polygon must be simple — its sides never cross. If the sides cross over each other (like a bowtie), it's self-intersecting and isn't considered a polygon at all!

🔮 Recap: A polygon is a simple closed shape made of straight sides. It's named by its number of sides (triangle = 3, up through decagon = 10). Interior angle sum = (n − 2) × 180°; exterior angle sum is always 360°; diagonals = n(n − 3) ÷ 2. Regular means equal sides & angles; convex means no dents; simple means no crossing sides.
