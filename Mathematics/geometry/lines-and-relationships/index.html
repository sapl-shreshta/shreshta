📌 Fun fact: Any two points are always collinear (you can always draw a line through just two dots) — the fun starts at 3 or more points!

☂️ All Roads Lead to One Point (Concurrent Lines) Try me!

When 3 or more lines all pass through the same point, we call them concurrent lines, and that shared point is the point of concurrence. Just like the spokes of an umbrella! Click below to add lines.

Real life: Open umbrella spokes, bicycle wheel spokes, and the lines on a spider's web all meet at one concurrent point!

🤝 How Do Two Lines Get Along? Try me!

Two lines on the same flat surface can behave in 3 different ways. Click each button to see (and hear about) how!

Two lines that cross each other at a point are called intersecting lines. That crossing spot is called the point of intersection!

✂️

Real life: Scissors, a big letter "X", and two roads crossing at a junction!
