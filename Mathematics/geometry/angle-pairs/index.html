Angle Pairs

When angles team up — complementary, supplementary, adjacent & vertically opposite!

📐

Hi, it's Ray the Ruler again 👋 Now that you know your angle types, let's see how angles behave when there's TWO of them together. Some team up to make special totals — let's find out!

🧩 Complementary Angles Drag both!

Two angles are complementary if they add up to exactly 90° — they don't even need to be touching! Drag each pink arm below and try to make them complementary.

📌 Complementary angles don't have to be next to each other — as long as the two measures add up to 90°, they're complementary!

🧩 Supplementary Angles Drag both!

Two angles are supplementary if they add up to exactly 180°. Same idea as complementary, just a bigger target!

📏 Angles on a Straight Line (Linear Pair) Drag C!

When one ray splits a straight line into two angles, those two angles are called a linear pair — and they ALWAYS add up to 180°, no matter where you drag the divider!

📌 A linear pair is always supplementary AND adjacent at the same time — it's the most common example of supplementary angles you'll see!

🔄 Angles Around a Point Try me!

However you slice up the full circle around a point, all the pieces always add up to 360°. Click below to add divider rays and see it happen!

Click "Add a Divider Ray" at least twice to start splitting the circle!

🔗 Adjacent Angles Drag C!

Two angles are adjacent if they share the same vertex AND a common arm, with their other arms pointing away from each other (no overlap). Drag the purple common arm OC below.

✅ Adjacent: ∠AOC and ∠COB sit side-by-side, sharing only arm OC — no overlap!

❌ Not adjacent: ∠COD is entirely INSIDE ∠COA — they overlap, sharing more than just one arm's worth of space.

✂️ Vertically Opposite Angles Drag the pink line!

When two straight lines cross, they make 4 angles. The two angles directly across from each other (like the top and bottom slice of an X) are called vertically opposite angles — and they're always equal!

✂️

Real life: Open scissors, an hourglass shape, and two crossing roads all show vertically opposite angles in action!

🔮 Recap: Complementary = sum to 90°. Supplementary = sum to 180°. A Linear Pair is a supplementary pair that's also adjacent, sitting on a straight line. Angles around a point sum to 360°. Adjacent = share a vertex + arm, no overlap. Vertically opposite = equal angles made by two crossing lines!
