Elements — The Building Blocks of Everything!

From gold to oxygen — let's explore what everything is made of 🚀

🌍 Everything is made of Matter!

Your chair, water, air, even you — all made of matter! 🤗

Matter is made of tiny building blocks. Scientists split matter into two big groups:

✅ Pure Substances

One type of particle — Elements & Compounds

🥣 Mixtures

Different particles mixed together

🔬 What is an Element?

⚡ An element is a pure substance that cannot be broken into simpler substances by any physical or chemical method. ⚡

An element is made of only one type of atom — the smallest particle that still has all the properties of that element.

🟡 Gold

Only gold atoms

💛 Hydrogen

Only hydrogen atoms

💙 Oxygen

Only oxygen atoms

🔢 How many Elements are there?

118

known elements in the universe today!

🌿 92

Found naturally on Earth

🧪 26

Made artificially by scientists

Examples: gold, silver, mercury, sulphur, hydrogen, oxygen, iron…

📋 Characteristics of an Element

  • Cannot be broken into simpler substances — it is the basic form of matter
  • Is a pure substance — contains atoms of only one kind
  • Can exist on its own or combine with other elements to form compounds
  • Exists in all three states — solid, liquid, and gas
  • Each element has a unique symbol of 1 or 2 letters

🗂️ Types of Elements

Elements are classified based on their properties into four groups:

🔩 Metals

Shiny, hard, conduct electricity

🌿 Non-Metals

Mostly gases or brittle solids

🔶 Metalloids

Properties of both!

💨 Noble Gases

Don't react with anything

🔩 Metals

Most of the 118 elements are metals! They are found mainly in the Earth's crust and in seas and oceans.

Examples: Gold Silver Iron Copper Aluminium Sodium Calcium

  • Usually hard solids with a shiny, lustrous appearance ✨
  • Good conductors of heat and electricity ⚡
  • High melting and boiling points 🌡️
  • Malleable (beaten into sheets) & ductile (drawn into wires)
  • Sonorous — produce a ringing sound when struck 🔔

🌿 Non-Metals

Non-metals are abundant in the Earth's crust, oceans, and atmosphere.

Examples: Hydrogen Oxygen Nitrogen Chlorine Sulphur Phosphorus

  • Soft solids or gases — mostly brittle (break easily) 💨
  • No shiny/lustrous appearance — dull surface
  • Bad conductors of heat and electricity
  • Neither malleable nor ductile
  • Low melting and boiling points & not sonorous

🔶 Metalloids — The Best of Both!

Metalloids have properties of both metals and non-metals. They are in-between! 🤝

Examples: Boron Silicon Arsenic Antimony

  • Solid — can be shiny or dull
  • Ductile and malleable (like metals)
  • Conduct heat and electricity better than non-metals, but not as well as metals
  • Silicon is used to make computer chips! 💻

💨 Noble (Inert) Gases

Noble gases are gaseous elements that do not react with any other elements — they are the lone wolves of chemistry! 🐺

Examples: Helium Neon Argon Krypton Xenon Radon

  • Present only in traces in the air
  • Helium fills balloons — lighter than air! 🎈
  • Neon glows beautifully in neon signs 💡
  • Completely stable — no need to share or bond!

🏔️

Earth's Crust

🌬️

Atmosphere

🧍

Human Body

🌟 Fun Facts about Elements!

  • Aluminium is the most common metal in the Earth's crust 🏔️
  • Iron makes up a large part of the Earth's core 🌍
  • Mercury is the only metal that is liquid at room temperature 🌡️
  • Bromine is the only non-metal that is liquid at room temperature 🧴
  • Hydrogen + Helium together = about 99% of the universe's mass 🌌
  • Metals are malleable (beaten into sheets) and ductile (drawn into wires) 🔧

🎉 Let's Recap! 🎉

  • An element cannot be broken into simpler substances ⚗️
  • There are 118 elements — 92 natural, 26 artificial
  • Elements are classified as Metals, Non-Metals, Metalloids, and Noble Gases
  • Metals are shiny, ductile, malleable, and conduct electricity ⚡
  • Non-metals are dull, brittle, and poor conductors
  • Metalloids are in between — like silicon in computer chips 💻
  • Noble gases don't react with anything — perfectly stable! 💨
