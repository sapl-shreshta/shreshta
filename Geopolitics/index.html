Geopolitics

How countries team up, compete and shape our world 🤝

What is geopolitics? It is the study of how geography (where a country is, what resources it has, who its neighbours are) and politics (power, money, friendships and rivalries) come together to decide what countries do. Why does India buy oil from one country and sell medicines to another? Why do some countries form clubs? That is geopolitics!
