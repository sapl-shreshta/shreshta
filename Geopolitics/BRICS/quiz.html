🔟 Top 10 facts to remember 🌱 BASIC

  1. India hosted the 18th BRICS Summit at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 12–13 September 2026.
  2. Theme: Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability (spells BRICS!).
  3. 2026 marked 20 years of BRICS, and India's 4th chairship (2012, 2016, 2021, 2026).
  4. BRICS has 11 members (per India's official list) and 10 partner countries.
  5. Main outcome: the New Delhi Declaration, about 140 paragraphs.
  6. Brazil's President Lula did not attend; Xi Jinping made his first India visit in about 7 years.
  7. BRICS backed UN Security Council reform; China & Russia supported a "greater role" for India and Brazil.
  8. No common currency — instead, local-currency payments and a Payment Task Force.
  9. New ideas: Grain Exchange (2027), Risk Lab at GIFT City, DPI Repository, Space Council, NIMHANS-led mental health network.
  10. China hosts the next summit in 2027.

✍️ Write it like a topper 🚀 ADVANCED

Question: Write a short note on the 18th BRICS Summit.

Try writing your own first, then open the model answer

The 18th BRICS Summit was held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 12–13 September 2026, under India's fourth chairship and the theme "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability." It marked 20 years of BRICS, which now has 11 members and 10 partner countries. Leaders adopted the New Delhi Declaration, organised around three pillars — political-security, economic-financial and people-to-people cooperation. Key themes included reform of the UN Security Council, IMF and World Bank, a stronger voice for the Global South, opposition to unilateral tariffs and sanctions, WTO reform, and local-currency payments (without a common currency). New initiatives included a BRICS Grain Exchange, a Risk Lab at GIFT City, a DPI Repository, a Space Council and a mental-health network led by NIMHANS. On conflicts, the Declaration called for "maximum restraint" in the Middle East, an end to the war in Gaza and a two-state solution, and dialogue on Ukraine. The summit showed India's ability to build consensus among diverse members while insisting BRICS is "not against anyone."

💡 Topper tips
  • Start with who, when, where, theme.
  • Group points under headings (governance, economy, technology, peace).
  • End with significance — why it matters for India.

🧠 Big-think challenges 🧠 PRO

No single right answer — these are for discussion at the dinner table!

  1. Be the sherpa: Iran wants the Declaration to condemn attacks on it by name. The UAE refuses. You're India's sherpa. Write one sentence both could sign.
  2. Currency debate: List 2 benefits and 2 dangers for India if BRICS created a common currency. Would you vote yes or no?
  3. Club size: Should BRICS keep adding members? Argue for in 3 lines, then against in 3 lines.
  4. Maths + geopolitics: If India–Russia trade must reach $100 billion by 2030 and is $70 billion in 2026, how much must it grow each year on average (simple, not compound)?
    Show answer

    $30 billion ÷ 4 years = $7.5 billion per year. (Made-up starting number for practice.)

  5. Compare sources: One set of notes says BRICS has 11 members; a news report says Saudi Arabia attended "as an invitee". Why might trustworthy sources disagree? How would you check?
  6. Future you: Pick one 2026 initiative (Grain Exchange, DPI Repository, Space Council, Startup Fund…). Imagine it's 2035 and it was a huge success. Write a newspaper headline about it! 📰
