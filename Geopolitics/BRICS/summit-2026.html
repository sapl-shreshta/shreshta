🔑 The theme: R-I-C-S 🌱 BASIC

India chose a very clever theme. Look at the first letters:

Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability

👉 B-R-I-C-S! The theme spells the name of the group. 🎉

🛡️

Resilience

Being able to bounce back from shocks: pandemics, price jumps, broken supply chains, climate disasters, energy shortages, money crises.

💡

Innovation

New ideas & tech: AI, quantum, robotics, startups, digital public infrastructure, scientific research.

🤝

Cooperation

Working together on trade, finance, health, education, energy, transport, youth and culture.

🌱

Sustainability

Growing without harming the planet: climate, renewable energy, forests, water, biodiversity.

🚀 ADVANCED: What does "resilience" really mean for a country?

During COVID-19, factories in one country shut down and suddenly the whole world ran short of chips, medicines and even shipping containers. A resilient country doesn't depend on only one supplier for important things. It's like not keeping all your eggs in one basket 🥚🧺. That's why BRICS talked about "resilient supply chains" and "critical minerals" (Chapter 3).

⭐ Why 2026 was special 🌱 BASIC

  • 🎂 20th anniversary — a chance to ask "What should BRICS become in the next 10 years?"
  • 🇮🇳 India's 4th chairship — India got to set the agenda.
  • 📈 The first summit where the expanded group really had to "work together" — moving from growing bigger to growing stronger. Experts called it a shift from "expansion" to "consolidation".
  • 🌪️ A stormy world: a war involving Iran in the Middle East, the war in Gaza, the Russia–Ukraine war, and new trade tariffs from the USA.
🧠 PRO: Why getting a Declaration at all was a win

In May 2026, BRICS foreign ministers met and could not agree on a joint statement. Iran and the UAE — both members — were on opposite sides of the Middle East conflict. So many experts doubted the leaders would agree either.

India managed to get all members to sign one declaration. That's why analysts called it a diplomatic success for India's "bridge-building" role.

💡 Did you know?

The International Solar Alliance and CDRI were both started by India! Inviting them was a smart way for India to show off its own global projects.

Does it matter if a president sends a minister instead of coming himself?

Yes — in diplomacy, who shows up sends a message. A leader's presence shows the meeting is a top priority. Lula's absence was explained by his election campaign (Brazil votes in October 2026), so most experts did not see it as a snub. But it did mean Brazil had a smaller voice in the leaders' room.

🗓️ A whole year of meetings 🚀 ADVANCED

The summit is just 2 days, but India's chairship lasted the whole year.

~30

Examples: Energy Ministers met in Gurugram under the theme "Energy for All"; finance, health, agriculture, education, youth and many other groups met in different cities.

Why spread meetings across 30 cities instead of holding them all in Delhi?
  • It shows off India's diversity — food, culture, heritage — to foreign visitors.
  • It brings business and tourism to many cities, not just the capital.
  • It makes ordinary citizens across India feel part of world events. India did the same during its G20 year in 2023.

📜 The New Delhi Declaration 🌱 BASIC

The BRICS New Delhi Declaration is the main result of the summit — a long document (about 140 paragraphs) listing everything the members agree on and want to do together.

📝 Think of it like…

…a class agreement signed by every student at the start of the year: what we believe, what we'll work on, and how we'll behave. It isn't a law — nobody gets punished for breaking it — but it shows what everyone publicly agreed to.

The big headlines (details in Chapters 3–5)

🏛️ Global governance

Reform the UN, UN Security Council, IMF & World Bank so developing countries get more say.

🚢 Trade

Opposed one-sided tariffs; support a reformed WTO.

💱 Money

Pay each other in local currencies. No common currency.

🤖 Technology

AI governance, digital public infrastructure, quantum, space, cyber.

🌾 Food & health

A BRICS Grain Exchange, mental-health network led by NIMHANS.

🕊️ Peace

"Maximum restraint" in the Middle East; Gaza; dialogue on Ukraine; zero tolerance for terrorism.

☕ Side meetings & events 🚀 ADVANCED

At big summits, a lot of important work happens in one-to-one ("bilateral") meetings on the side.

11 Sep · Modi 🤝 Putin

Talked trade, energy and defence. Repeated the goal of $100 billion India–Russia trade by 2030.

11 Sep · BRICS Business Forum

Business leaders, banks and startups met. Modi asked members to find and remove barriers to trade between BRICS countries, help startups grow, and keep sea trade routes safe.

11–12 Sep · "Bharat Innovates" Expo

Showcased 37 Indian deep-tech startups (deep-tech = hard science like AI, space, biotech).

12 Sep · Modi 🤝 Xi

Talked about keeping the border calm and the trade imbalance (India buys far more from China than it sells). Experts saw it as a careful step toward steadier relations.

12 Sep · Iran 🤝 UAE

The highest-level Iran–UAE meeting since the 2026 war started — BRICS gave rivals a place to talk.

🚀 ADVANCED: What is a "trade imbalance"?

If you buy ₹100 of stickers from your friend but she buys only ₹20 of your stickers, there's an imbalance of ₹80. India buys much more from China (phones, electronics, machines) than it sells to China. India wants China to buy more Indian goods to make it fairer.

🎯 What India wanted from its chairship 🚀 ADVANCED

  • 1️⃣ A stronger Global South — more say for developing countries in world institutions.
  • 2️⃣ Technology cooperation — AI, digital public infrastructure, quantum, cybersecurity.
  • 3️⃣ Economic cooperation — trade, payments in local currencies, MSMEs, supply chains.
  • 4️⃣ Development — health, education, infrastructure, climate, skills.
  • 5️⃣ Make BRICS practical — not just speeches, but projects + institutions + technology + finance.
🧠 PRO: Soft power

India used the summit to show the world its own successes — UPI, Aadhaar, GIFT City, NIMHANS, the International Solar Alliance, Andhra Pradesh MedTech Zone. When other countries admire and copy your ideas, you gain influence without force. This is called "soft power".

🎬 Behind the scenes & debates at home 🧠 PRO

Hosting world leaders is a huge job:

  • 👮 About 30,000 security personnel were on duty.
  • 🏫 Schools, many offices and even the Supreme Court closed on 11 September; roads were diverted.

In a democracy, opposition parties question the government's choices — even during big international events:

  • The Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) criticised the government for putting screens around poorer neighbourhoods, saying it hid poverty instead of fixing it.
  • The Congress party criticised the all-vegetarian gala dinner for foreign guests.
Why is it healthy that opposition parties criticise the government, even during a summit?

In a democracy the opposition's job is to hold the government accountable — to ask questions and point out problems. You don't have to agree with every criticism, but the fact that people can speak openly is a sign of a free society. (Link this to your Civics lessons!)
