🤖 Artificial Intelligence 🌱 BASIC

AI means computer systems that can do things that normally need human thinking — understanding language, recognising pictures, making predictions. AI was one of the biggest themes of 2026.

✅ AI opportunities

  • Economic growth
  • Scientific discoveries
  • Better farming, health & education
  • Sustainable development

⚠️ What BRICS wants AI to be

  • Safe & secure
  • Reliable & trustworthy
  • Inclusive & accessible to all
  • Energy-efficient
  • With proper risk management
  • BRICS committed to carry out its Leaders' Statement on the Global Governance of AI (agreed at the 2025 Rio summit).
  • The Declaration praised India's AI Impact Summit, held in New Delhi in February 2026.
🚀 ADVANCED: What is "AI governance"?

Governance = the rules and systems for managing something. AI governance asks questions like:

  • Who is responsible if an AI makes a harmful mistake?
  • How do we stop AI being used for cheating, scams or weapons?
  • Should only a few rich countries and companies control the most powerful AI?

BRICS wants developing countries to help write these rules, not just follow rules written by others.

🧠 PRO: Why "energy-efficient" AI?

Training and running big AI models uses giant data centres that need huge amounts of electricity and water for cooling. For countries still working to give everyone electricity, wasteful AI is a real concern. Can you think of a way AI could actually save energy? (e.g., smarter power grids, better weather forecasts for solar farms)

📱 Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) 🌱 BASIC

Infrastructure usually means roads, bridges, railways. Digital public infrastructure is the same idea but online: shared digital "roads" that the government builds so that everyone — banks, companies, citizens — can use them cheaply.

🪪

Aadhaar

Digital identity for over a billion Indians.

💸

UPI

Instant phone payments — even ₹5 at a tea stall!

📂

DigiLocker

Your certificates and documents stored safely online.

💉

CoWIN

Booked and recorded COVID vaccinations for crores of people.

In 2026: BRICS created a BRICS DPI Repository — a shared collection of digital public goods other countries can learn from and copy — and explored pilot projects between members.

🚀 ADVANCED: Why DPI is a big deal for the Global South

Building a new system for every service is expensive. DPI is like building one good road that every bus company can use, instead of each company building its own road. It lets countries serve huge populations at low cost. That's why India is proudly sharing its model — it's a good example of India's soft power.

🔐 Cybersecurity & deepfakes 🌱 BASIC

BRICS discussed online dangers:

🦠 Malicious software

Viruses and programs that steal data or lock computers.

🕵️ Cybercrime

Online fraud, hacking, stealing money.

🗞️ Misinformation & disinformation

Misinformation = false info spread by mistake. Disinformation = false info spread on purpose.

🎭 Deepfakes

AI-made fake videos or voices that look real.

BRICS supports cooperation between national cybersecurity teams, annual cyber exercises (practice drills), and responsible behaviour by countries in cyberspace.

🛡️ Be your own cyber-guard

Before believing or sharing a shocking video: Stop → Check the source → Look for the same news on trusted sites → Ask an adult. That's what cyber-experts do too!

⚛️ Quantum technology 🚀 ADVANCED

Normal computers use bits — each is either 0 or 1, like a light switch that's off or on. 💡

Quantum computers use qubits, which follow the strange rules of the tiny world of atoms. A qubit can be in a mix of 0 and 1 at the same time (called superposition) — more like a spinning coin 🪙 that is both heads and tails until it lands.

For certain problems — designing new medicines, new materials, cracking codes — quantum computers could be far faster than today's computers.

BRICS in 2026: agreed to cooperate on quantum research, share knowledge, and proposed a wider BRICS research infrastructure network.

🧠 PRO: Why do governments care?

Most online security (bank passwords, messages) relies on maths problems that normal computers take forever to solve. A powerful quantum computer might solve them quickly — so countries are racing to build quantum-safe security. Technology is also a matter of national security.

🛰️ Space & undersea cables 🚀 ADVANCED

🚀

BRICS Space Council

A new body to coordinate space cooperation among members.

🛰️

Remote-sensing satellites

BRICS members share images from their Earth-watching satellites (a "constellation"). It was expanded in 2026. Used for farming, floods, forests and disasters.

🌊

Submarine cables

A feasibility study (a check whether it's possible and worth it) for a BRICS undersea internet cable network.

💡 Mind-blowing fact

Almost all the internet data that travels between continents goes through cables lying on the ocean floor — not satellites! When you watch a video hosted in another country, it probably zoomed under the sea. 🐟📡 Owning or sharing such cables means more control over your own internet.

🏥 Health & mental health 🌱 BASIC

BRICS talked about universal health coverage (good healthcare for everyone), vaccines, diagnostics (tests), medicines, health workers, medical research and preparing for the next pandemic.

🧠

Mental health network

A BRICS network of Centres of Excellence in mental health, with India's NIMHANS (Bengaluru) as the coordinating centre — including a training hub.

🩺

BRICS BioDesign Centre

Launched by the Andhra Pradesh MedTech Zone (Visakhapatnam) — for designing medical devices.

💻

Digital health

A Compendium on Digital Health Architecture for Continuum of Care — a guidebook on how digital records can follow a patient from clinic to hospital to home.

Why would countries work together on pandemic preparedness?

Viruses don't need passports! 🦠✈️ COVID-19 showed that a disease in one place can reach the whole world in weeks. Sharing data, vaccines and research early can save millions of lives.

⚡ Energy 🚀 ADVANCED

BRICS is an unusual mix: it has some of the world's biggest oil & gas sellers (Russia, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Iran) and some of the biggest buyers (China, India).

  • India's BRICS Energy Ministers' meeting was held in Gurugram with the theme "Energy for All" — focusing on energy security, sustainability, innovation and access for everyone.
  • A forum on Sustainable Aviation Fuels — cleaner fuel for planes, made from things like used cooking oil or farm waste. ✈️🌿
🧠 PRO: A built-in tension

Oil sellers want high oil prices; oil buyers want low ones. Oil sellers may want the world to keep using oil longer; buyers like India want cheap, secure energy and more renewables. Getting them to agree on energy language is a real diplomatic puzzle!

🌡️ Climate change 🌱 BASIC

BRICS reaffirmed support for:

  • The Paris Agreement (2015) — the world's plan to keep global warming well below 2°C.
  • The UNFCCC — the UN body where climate deals are negotiated every year at the "COP" meetings.
  • Mitigation (reducing pollution), adaptation (preparing for heatwaves, floods), and loss and damage (helping countries recover from climate disasters).

BRICS stressed that developing countries need more climate finance, technology and capacity building (training and skills).

🚀 ADVANCED: "Common but differentiated responsibilities"

This is an important climate idea: everyone must fight climate change (common), but countries that polluted more in the past and are richer should do more and pay more (differentiated).

Imagine a class picnic that left a big mess. Everyone should help clean — but the students who made most of the mess, and who have the most cleaning supplies, should do the most. 🧹

🌳 Forests, water & big cats 🌱 BASIC

  • 🌳 Protecting forests, especially tropical forests (Brazil's Amazon, Indonesia's rainforests).
  • 🏜️ Fighting land degradation, desertification and drought.
  • 🌿 Mangroves — coastal forests that protect shores from storms.
  • 💧 Water and biodiversity finance (money to protect nature).
  • 🌱 Adopted principles for "integrated landscape-based greening" — protecting whole landscapes (forests + farms + rivers) together.
🐅 Big cats!

India promoted cooperation on conserving big cats. India launched the International Big Cat Alliance in 2023 for the world's seven big cats:

🐅 Tiger · 🦁 Lion · 🐆 Leopard · ❄️ Snow leopard · 🐆 Cheetah · 🐆 Jaguar · 🐈 Puma

Fun fact: India has about three-quarters of the world's wild tigers, and brought cheetahs back to Kuno National Park in 2022.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Youth & people 🌱 BASIC

The third pillar is about people, not just governments. In 2026 BRICS adopted the BRICS Youth Cooperation Framework 2026 and supported exchanges in education, sports, culture, tourism, women's empowerment and academic research.

🧠 Think about it

Why would governments care about young people from different countries meeting each other? (Hint: today's students become tomorrow's leaders, scientists and business owners. Friendships formed young can make countries trust each other more later.)
