यह क्या है?

Hindi · Worksheet 1 · What is this?

✨ आज का जादुई वाक्य · Today's magic sentence

यह ? है।
“This is ___.”  Put any thing in the gap — it always works!
🍎 यह सेब है।
📖 यह किताब है।
🐱 यह बिल्ली है।

Someone asks: यह क्या है? (What is this?) — and you answer with the whole sentence, not just one word. 💪

1️⃣ मिलाओ · Match

Tap a picture, then tap its Hindi word. Read the word aloud when you match it!

2️⃣ खाली जगह भरो · Fill the gap

Tap a word from the box, then tap the gap where it belongs. Then press 🔊 and say the sentence with it.

3️⃣ पहेली बूझो · Riddles

Read the riddle (or press 🔊). Who am I? Choose the answer as a full sentence. Stuck? Tap 🔍 for the English meaning.

4️⃣ बोलो! · Say it!

Look at the picture, read the word, and say the whole sentence out loud: यह ___ है। Then press 🔊 to check!
