🌸 తాత్పర్యం · What Happens in the Poem

The monkeys (వానరులు) are building a bridge (సేతువు) across the ocean to Lanka. They carry huge rocks and trees and hand them to Nala, who fits them into the bridge.

A tiny squirrel thinks, "The bridge must be finished quickly — I will help these mighty ones too!" Keeping Sri Rama's lotus feet in its heart, it keeps doing this again and again:

🌊 dips in the sea→🏖️ rolls in the sand→🏃 runs to the bridge→🪨 shakes the sand between the rocks→🔁 comes back again

Sri Rama sees it from far away and says, "Lakshmana, look! While the monkey chiefs bring mountains with great strength, this little squirrel never thinks 'how small am I?' — it helps with love. Did you see?"

Lakshmana replies, "I see it, brother. For anyone who keeps your feet in their heart, even a blade of grass becomes as great as the mountain of the gods. So it is devotion that truly matters."

Rama is delighted. He asks Sugriva to bring the squirrel, takes it in his hand, praises it in many ways, and gently strokes its back with the fingers of his right hand. That is how the squirrel got the three beautiful lines on its back 🐿️✨ — and we still see them today!

🗣️ In very simple Telugu

వానరులు సముద్రంపై వారధి కడుతున్నారు. ఒక చిన్న ఉడుత కూడా సాయం చేయాలనుకుంది. నీటిలో మునిగి, ఇసుకలో పొర్లి, ఆ ఇసుకను వారధి రాళ్ల మధ్య రాల్చింది. శ్రీరాముడు అది చూసి సంతోషించాడు. ఉడుతను చేతిలోకి తీసుకుని ప్రేమగా వీపు నిమిరాడు. అప్పటి నుండి ఉడుత వీపుపై మూడు గీతలు ఉన్నాయి.

📚 ముఖ్యమైన పదాలు · Key Words to Learn

శక్తి కాదు, భక్తి ముఖ్యం! It's not how big your help is — it's how sincerely you give it. 💛
That's why we still say "ఉడుత సాయం" for a small help given with a big heart.
