THE BIG IDEA

Three ways a substance can treat light

🥛 Transparent

Light passes through easily. You can see objects clearly and distinctly on the other side.

GlassAirWaterClear plastic

🧊 Translucent

Only some light gets through. Shapes and colours blur into a faint impression — not a clear picture.

Frosted glassTracing paperButter paperThin curtain

🪵 Opaque

No light gets through at all. Whatever is behind it is completely hidden — a shadow forms.

WoodMetalBrick wallCardboard

BEFORE YOU GO

Three things to remember

🥛

Transparent = passes through easily

Glass, air and water let light travel through with almost nothing lost, so you see clearly across them.

🧊

Translucent = partly blocked

Frosted glass and tracing paper scatter the light as it passes, so only a blurry glow gets through.

🪵

Opaque = fully blocked

Wood and metal stop light completely, casting a shadow on whatever is behind them.
