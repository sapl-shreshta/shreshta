THE BIG IDEA

Light is a form of energy

Heat is energy that warms things. Sound is energy that makes things vibrate. Light is energy too — a kind that travels incredibly fast and, when it reaches your eye, gives you the sensation of vision. That's the whole job of light: it doesn't just light things up, it lets you see.

⚡ How fast is fast?

300,000 km/s

Light could race around the entire Earth about 7 times in one second. That's why flipping a switch seems to light up a room instantly — the light has already crossed it before you finish blinking.

BEFORE YOU GO

Three things to remember

⚡

Light is energy

It's a form of energy that produces the sensation of vision when it reaches your eye — just like heat and sound are forms of energy for touch and hearing.

🙈

Light itself is invisible

You never see a beam of light travelling through empty space. You only see it the instant it hits something and reflects.

👁️

Seeing = reflected light reaching your eye

Objects become visible when light bounces off them and lands on your eye. No bounce reaching your eye, no image — however bright the room is.
