MATTER

Everything in the Universe 🚀

❓ What is Matter?

Matter is defined as anything that occupies space and has mass.

It can be perceived by our senses: sight, touch, smell, taste, and hearing! 👀👃👂

  • Can be perceived by our senses
  • Occupies physical space
  • Has measurable mass
  • Is classified as living or non-living

📦 Examples of Matter

💨 GASES

Oxygen, Hydrogen, Carbon, Air

💧 LIQUIDS

Water, Oil, Milk

🪨 SOLIDS

Food, Clothes, Steel, Wood

  • 🌬️ Air
  • 💧 Water

⚛️ Atoms & Molecules

Democritus (Greek) called the smallest particle an 🌟 atom 🌟

John Dalton discovered that atoms CANNOT exist freely in nature!

Instead, matter is made of MOLECULES

Fun Fact: A small drop of water contains HUNDREDS OF MILLIONS of atoms! 💧

🔍 Characteristics of Particles

The particles of matter have 4 KEY CHARACTERISTICS:

  • They are very small in size
  • They have spaces between them
  • They are in constant random motion
  • They always attract each other

Let's explore each characteristic in detail! 👇

1️⃣ Tiny Particles

Particles of matter are EXTREMELY SMALL - invisible to naked eye!

🧪 Potassium Permanganate Experiment:

Take a pink crystal → dissolve in water → deep purple solution! 🟣

Dilute more → lighter color!

Keep diluting → color gets even lighter!

Why? One crystal breaks into MILLIONS of tiny particles!

2️⃣ Spaces Between Particles

All matter has intermolecular space - gaps between particles!

  • All particles have space between them
  • Size varies by state of matter
  • Explains why objects compress
  • Allows substances to mix and diffuse

Example: When sugar dissolves in water, particles spread into the spaces! ✨

3️⃣ Constant Random Motion

Particles of matter are NEVER AT REST!

They move randomly in all directions in a zigzag pattern! 〰️

  • Always moving randomly
  • Follow zigzag paths
  • Speed varies with temperature
  • Faster when hot, slower when cold 🔥❄️

Evidence: Brownian motion & Diffusion! 👀

4️⃣ Intermolecular Forces

Particles ATTRACT EACH OTHER!

This force keeps particles together in matter! 🤝

Strength Varies! 💪

STRONG force: Particles held tightly → hard to break 🪨

WEAK force: Particles loosely bound → easy to break 🎨

Nanoparticle: A cluster of several molecules together! ⭐

🔀 Three States of Matter

🪨 SOLID

Steel, Rock, Ice

💧 LIQUID

Water, Oil, Milk

💨 GAS

Oxygen, Steam, Air

✨ Fun Fact: Water can exist in ALL 3 states! ❄️ → 💧 → 💨 ✨

🪨 Solids

VERY STRONG intermolecular forces!

NEGLIGIBLE (almost no) intermolecular space!

  • Definite shape and size ✓
  • Cannot be compressed ✓
  • Cannot flow ✓
  • Highly dense & closely packed
  • Low thermal expansion
  • Does not easily diffuse into other solids

Molecules vibrate on either side of their main positions!

💧 Liquids

WEAK intermolecular forces!

More intermolecular space than solids!

  • Definite volume BUT no definite shape
  • Acquire shape of container 📦
  • Can flow easily 🌊
  • Almost incompressible
  • Viscous (thickness varies)
  • High thermal expansion
  • Exert pressure in ALL directions! 🔄

💨 Gases

VERY WEAK or NEGLIGIBLE forces!

FAR MORE intermolecular space than liquids!

  • NO definite shape OR volume! 🌊
  • Acquire shape & volume of container
  • Highly compressible 🤏
  • Can flow easily
  • Diffuse into other gases VERY easily
  • Very large thermal expansion 🔥
  • Exert pressure on ALL container walls! 💥

🔥 Melting (Solid → Liquid)

Solid changes to liquid by absorbing HEAT at melting point!

How It Works:

  • Heat increases vibration amplitude 📈
  • Particles gain enough energy ⚡
  • Overcome attractive forces 💪
  • Become FREE TO MOVE → Liquid! 💧

Example: Ice melting into water! ❄️ → 💧

☁️ Vaporization (Liquid → Gas)

Liquid changes to gas by absorbing HEAT!

How It Works:

  • Heat increases molecular energy ⚡
  • Molecules move VERY rapidly 💨
  • Overcome holding forces 💪
  • Escape into gaseous state! ☁️

Example: Water boiling! 💧 → 🌫️

Key Takeaways!

  • Matter is anything that occupies space and has mass
  • Particles are extremely small and ALWAYS in motion
  • 3 states: Solid, Liquid, Gas (determined by forces & spaces)
  • Heat causes phase transitions through particle energy changes
  • Understanding matter helps us understand the UNIVERSE! 🌌
