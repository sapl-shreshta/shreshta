📏 The Big Idea

What is Measurement?

Measurement is comparing something unknown with a known fixed amount. We do it all the time!

🍎Buying fruitweighed on a scale
✂️Stitching clothesa tailor measures you
🤒Feeling sicka doctor checks your temp
⚖️Comparing thingswhich is bigger?
Every measurement has two parts:
🔢 Magnitude — the number
📐 Unit — the standard

03
🧩 Two Teams

Fundamental vs Derived

🧱
Fundamental

The building blocks. They don't depend on anything else. There are 7 of them!

  • Length, Mass, Time
  • Temperature, Electric Current
  • Luminous Intensity
  • Amount of Substance
🏗️
Derived

Built by combining the building blocks together!

  • Area = Length × Length
  • Volume = Length × Length × Length
  • Speed = Distance ÷ Time
  • Density = Mass ÷ Volume
04
🪄 The Magic Formula

How do we write a measurement?

Every measurement needs a number AND a unit. Put them together like this:

P = N × U
📦Pthe Physical quantity
🔢Nthe Number (magnitude)
📐Uthe Unit
🪣
A bucket weighs 2 kg → 2 is the number, kg is the unit. Easy! ✅

✍️ The Golden Rules

How to Write SI Units the Right Way

✅
DO this
  • Write names in small letters: metre, newton
  • Use plural names: "5 metres"
  • Stick prefixes on: mg, km, cm
🚫
DON'T do this
  • No full stop after symbols: 5 m, not 5 m.
  • Never make symbols plural: 5 m, not 5 ms
  • Symbol is capital only from a name: N for newton
Tip: the unit's name is lowercase, but its symbol can be capital if it's named after a scientist. 🧠

🔍 Tiny & Huge

Prefixes: from teeny to giant

Prefixes help us measure very small or very big things without huge numbers.

🐜milli (m)1 / 1000 · 1 mm = 0.001 m
🪙centi (c)1 / 100 · 1 cm = 0.01 m
📐deci (d)1 / 10 · 1 dm = 0.1 m
🚶deca (da)10 times bigger
🏢hecto (h)100 times bigger
🏔️kilo (k)1000 times · 1 km = 1000 m
🪜
Think of a staircase: each step up is ×10, each step down is ÷10.

📏 Measuring Length

How long is it? SI unit: metre (m)

🧰 Tools for the job
📏 Ruler
📐 Metre rod
🧵 Measuring tape
🔧 Vernier callipers

The smallest line on a ruler is 1 millimetre!

👁️ Use it correctly
1Lay the ruler flat & parallel to the object
2Line up the zero mark with one end
3Look straight down — sideways eyes cause a parallax error!
🤔
Broken ruler? Start at the 1 cm mark, end at 6 cm → length = 6 − 1 = 5 cm. Clever!

🟦 Measuring Area

Area = the space inside a shape

SI unit: square metre (m²). Use a formula for each shape:

⬜Squareside × side
▭Rectanglelength × breadth
🔺Triangle½ × base × height
✏️ Try one together!
A rectangular sheet of paper is 30 cm long and 25 cm wide.
30 × 25 = 750 cm²

⏰ Measuring Time

Time = the gap between two events

SI unit: second (s). From a blink to a whole century!

60 s = 1 min
60 min = 1 hour
24 hours = 1 day
100 years = 1 century
🏺 Long ago
☀️ Sundial
⏳ Sand clock
📱 Today
🕰️ Clock
🔢 Digital
⏱️ Stopwatch
🏁
A stopwatch can measure time as tiny as 0.01 seconds — perfect for races! And railways use the 24-hour clock (16:15 = 4:15 p.m.).
13
🌡️ Measuring Temperature

How hot or cold is it?

SI unit: kelvin (K). We measure with a thermometer filled with mercury or alcohol.

🔬 How it works
🔥Hot? The liquid expands and rises up the tube
❄️Cold? The liquid shrinks and falls down

The level of the liquid points to the temperature on the markings.

🤒 Body check-ups

A clinical thermometer measures your body (35–42°C). A little kink stops the mercury sliding back so the doctor can read it.

🌡️ Normal body = 37°C
